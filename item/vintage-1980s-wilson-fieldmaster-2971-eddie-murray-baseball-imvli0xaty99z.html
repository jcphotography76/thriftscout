Vintage 1980s Wilson Fieldmaster 2971 Eddie Murray Baseball Glove, Right-Hand Thrower

$29.99

This is a classic 1980s Wilson Fieldmaster model 2971 with the Eddie Murray signature stamp in the palm. Murray was a Hall of Fame first baseman and an Orioles legend, and these gloves were everywhere in his prime. It’s made of Select American Cowhide, with Wilson’s “Snap Action” design, a basket-weave web, the yellow “W” logo patch, and the classic Wilson script on the back. The leather has been cleaned and conditioned and is soft and broken in, with a rich brown color. The laces are intact and tied. It’s in good vintage condition, with normal wear for its age: The interior lining is mostly worn away, with some remaining in the fingers (see photos). There’s pocket darkening in the palm from use. There’s light surface scuffing and creasing on the back. It makes a great nostalgic piece for Orioles fans and glove collectors, or a solid candidate for relining if you want to play with it.
