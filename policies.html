Shipping and Returns

Shipping

  • We ship within the United States only.
  • Every order is shipped with tracking and insurance.
  • Shipping is added at checkout, based on the size and weight of the package. Want a quote before you buy? Email thriftscout.official@gmail.com.

Returns

We do not accept returns for fit or change of mind. Every item is secondhand and one of a kind, so please check the photos, measurements and condition notes before you buy.

If your item never arrives, arrives damaged, or is not as described, contact us within 3 days of delivery with photos. Refunds for damage or loss are made after the item is returned or the carrier approves a claim.
