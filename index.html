Our Mission

Bring thrifters together with a tool that just works

Thrift Scout exists to give every thrifter a fair shot at a good deal. Resellers hunting for margin, parents checking a price tag, collectors chasing a piece they have been missing: they all deserve a clear answer without paying more than the item is worth.

We are independent and veteran-owned, and we are building this to stay affordable while the community grows around it. Thrift Scout is young. Real feedback from real thrifters shapes what we build next, and once we are sustainable the goal is to give back to the community that makes this worthwhile.

To our military families: thank you. Whether you shop the thrift store down the road or the one on post, Thrift Scout is here to help you spot a fair price and share a find with a friend who needs it.

Know before you buy. Shop smart. Never get gouged.

For Resellers

Estimated resale value, profit after your cost, and comparison links, so you only haul home what earns.

For Families

A kid spots a toy for $4. Is that a good price? Check it in seconds, even if you never plan to resell.

For Collectors

Keep a personal Collection of what you love, with its estimated value, separate from what you plan to sell.

For Military Families

Check prices at base and post thrift stores, then share a find with a friend who needs a TV or a piece of furniture.

The Field Guide

How it works

Snap

Take a photo of the item, or upload a few. Enter the price on the tag.

Scan

The AI estimates what it is worth and returns a verdict: flip it, watch it, or pass.

Decide

Check the comparison links, run a careful re-check if you want a second opinion, then buy or walk away.

Save

Send it to your Sell Vault or your Collection, and share it with a friend if it is perfect for them.

So Thrift Scout opens like a real app, one tap from your phone's home screen.

iPhone

  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

  1. Open thriftscout.shop in Chrome.

Questions

Frequently asked

What is Thrift Scout?

A web app that helps you decide, right there in the aisle, whether a secondhand item is a good deal. Snap a photo, and it gives you an estimated resale value, comparison links, and a quick call: flip it, keep an eye on it, or pass.

Do I need to be a reseller?

Not at all. Resellers use it to find profit. Everyone else uses it to answer a simpler question: is $4 a fair price for this? Parents, collectors, bargain hunters and curious kids are all welcome.

How do I use it?

Create a free account, add your Gemini key in Settings, then tap Snap Photo (or Upload Photos). Enter the price on the tag, and run the scan. You get an estimate and a verdict. Save it to your Sell Vault if you plan to sell it, or to your Collection if you are keeping it.

What are the Sell Vault and the Collection?

Two shelves for your finds. The Sell Vault holds items you plan to sell, with estimated profit. The Collection holds the things you are keeping, with estimated value. You can move any item between them with one tap.

What does it cost?

Thrift Scout is in early testing and built to stay affordable. The scans run on Google's Gemini AI using your own free key, so there is no per-scan fee from us. Plans for the future will be announced here first. Google decides how many free scans a key gets each day and can change that, so we can't promise a number.

How do I get a Gemini key?

It takes about two minutes and is free from Google. Follow the step-by-step guide further down this page, then paste the key into Settings in the app. The key stays on your own device. Free keys come with daily limits set by Google, not by Thrift Scout. Those limits can change at any time, and if you reach one, scans pause until Google resets it.

How accurate are the values?

They are estimates, not appraisals or guarantees. Secondhand prices move with demand, condition and season. Use the number and the comparison links as a starting point, then trust your own eyes. There is also a careful re-check button for a second opinion on any saved item.

Is my Vault private?

Yes. You sign in with your own account, and your Vault and Collection are saved under it, separate from everyone else's. Still, Thrift Scout is in early testing, so avoid putting anything sensitive in item notes.

What are the ranks for?

Every scan and saved item earns XP, and XP moves you up through the Army enlisted ranks, Private through Sergeant Major of the Army. Each rank unlocks new colors and themes for the app. It is there to make the hunt more fun.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, it is built for phones. Open the site in your browser, then use the browser menu to Add to Home Screen. It will sit with your other apps and open full screen.

How do I send feedback or report a problem?

Email us using the Contact link at the bottom of this page. Tell us what phone you use and what happened. Feedback from early testers shapes what we build next.

It said the AI is busy. What now?

Sometimes Google's AI is slammed with requests and asks you to try again. Wait a few seconds and retry. Your photos are kept, so you will not need to retake them.

Get Started

Get your free Gemini key

Thrift Scout uses Google's Gemini AI to read your photos. You bring your own free key, which keeps scans in your hands and costs us nothing to run for you. It takes about two minutes.

  1. Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Tap Create API key. If it asks you to pick a project, choose any one or let it make a new one.
  3. Copy the long key that appears.
  4. Open Thrift Scout, go to Settings, tap Gemini API Key, paste it in and save.
  5. Go to the Scout tab and run your first scan.

Keep your key private and do not post it publicly. Free keys come with daily limits that Google sets, not Thrift Scout, and Google can change them at any time. If you hit your limit, scans pause until Google resets it. Thrift Scout stores your key only on your own device.
